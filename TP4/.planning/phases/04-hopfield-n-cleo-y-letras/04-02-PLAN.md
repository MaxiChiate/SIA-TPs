## Phase Goal

**As a** student, **I want to** validate a Hopfield core on four 5x5 letters, **so that** I understand recovery, energy, capacity and spurious states before moving to QR codes.

<objective>

Purpose: HOP-02 needs letters, D-01 and D-02 fix how they are encoded and checked, and D-04b moves the drawing of the real alphabet to the rule checkpoint, so this plan ships only the machinery.

</objective>
