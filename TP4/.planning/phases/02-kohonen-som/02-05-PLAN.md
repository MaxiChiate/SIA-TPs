## Phase Goal

**As a** campaign analyst, **I want to** train a Kohonen map of the 50 creators with a grid chosen by a rule fixed beforehand, **so that** I can read creator profiles by region and contrast them with PC1/PC2.

<objective>
Draw the chosen map. The U-matrix and adjacency are added to analysis/som.py and tested against a hand-computed grid; analysis/som_figures.py draws the hit map with the U-matrix, the component planes (trained variables in z units, excluded variables as raw means per neuron), the stage-1 curves that show why QE alone cannot choose, and the training trace. A make target regenerates them byte for byte and section 5 of the report explains how to read them.

Purpose: SOM-04 asks for the hit map, the U-matrix and the component planes of the chosen grid; plan 02-06 reads regions off the same run.
Output: analysis/som.py additions, analysis/som_figures.py, tests, make som-figures, figures under analysis/results/som/figures/, deliverable section 5.
</objective>
