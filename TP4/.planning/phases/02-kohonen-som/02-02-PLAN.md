## Phase Goal

**As a** campaign analyst, **I want to** train a Kohonen map of the 50 creators with a grid chosen by a rule fixed beforehand, **so that** I can read creator profiles by region and contrast them with PC1/PC2.

<objective>
Build the Python side of the SOM. First the reader: analysis/som.py reads a run directory of plan 02-01 and recomputes in numpy every measure the C binary reports (SOM-02), so the C numbers are proven before anything aggregates them. Then the two measures the grid rule needs and the C binary does not give: seed stability (D-13, tie-aware Spearman of grid-distance matrices) and QE leave-one-out (D-09, 50 trainings per seed and config, orchestrated from Python). Stability and LOO are proven on synthetic data and on the 6-row fixture; the cross-check trains two short configs on the real data. Nothing here runs the real stage-1 series (that is plan 02-04, after the rule is committed).

Purpose: the rule's filters and tie-break (plan 02-03) read QE, TE, occupancy, QE-LOO and stability per k; a C measure that numpy cannot reproduce, a stability that depends on creator order or a LOO that collides on run directories would make the choice arbitrary.
Output: analysis/som.py (reader, recompute, stability), analysis/tests/test_som.py, the analysis/README.md smoke-series row, analysis/som_loo.py with a CLI, analysis/tests/test_som_stats.py.
</objective>
