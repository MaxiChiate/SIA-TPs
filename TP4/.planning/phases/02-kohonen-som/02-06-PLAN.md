## Phase Goal

**As a** campaign analyst, **I want to** train a Kohonen map of the 50 creators with a grid chosen by a rule fixed beforehand, **so that** I can read creator profiles by region and contrast them with PC1/PC2.

<objective>
Turn the chosen map into answers. The committed region rule cuts the map into regions, each profiled in original units; the pre-declared statistics measure how far the map's layout follows PC1 and PC2; the highest U-matrix edges are read in terms of the variables that jump across them. Section 6 of the report interprets the regions for the agency and answers the two literal questions of the assignment; section 7 says how to regenerate everything.

Purpose: SOM-05 is the phase's user-facing outcome: profiles by region, contrasted with PC1/PC2 with numbers, and the written U-matrix answer. Phase 3 reads each creator's region for the campaigns.
Output: analysis/som_profiles.py with tests, make som-profiles, outputs under analysis/results/som/profiles/, deliverable sections 6 and 7, analysis/README.md.
</objective>
