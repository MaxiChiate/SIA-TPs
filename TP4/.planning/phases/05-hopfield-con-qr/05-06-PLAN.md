<objective>
Prepare the five 37x37 QR codes for the network: a deterministic PNG to pm-one converter producing datos/prepared/qr_pm1.csv (the preparation half of HOP-04). Split out of Plan 01 so each plan stays inside the per-plan context budget; this plan shares no files with Plan 01 and neither depends on the other, so both run in wave 1.

Purpose: the converter is the input boundary of the whole phase - the five PNGs become the ±1 matrix that the guard (Plan 02) hashes and the drivers (Plans 03-05) store. It must be byte-deterministic and must assert the measured 0/255 structure so a silently resampled or thresholded input can never slip into a run.

Output: scripts/qr.py (side-effect-free library) plus scripts/prepare_qr.py (CLI) plus a make qr target producing datos/prepared/qr_pm1.csv (generated, not versioned) and qr_pm1.meta.json with per-PNG sha256.

Flagged assumptions (surfaced, not resolved - no phase SPEC Edge Coverage):
- HOP-04 (polarity/threshold): dark pixel (0) maps to +1, light (255) maps to -1, documented in the meta; values asserted in {0,255} with no thresholding and no resampling (the PNGs are already 37x37, measured). Polarity does not change Hebb's W (global-flip invariance, Pitfall 22) and is fixed only for display.
</objective>
