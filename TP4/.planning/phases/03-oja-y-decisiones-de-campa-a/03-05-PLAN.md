## Phase Goal

**As a** campaign analyst, **I want to** build an Oja index and recommend creators per campaign against a followers-only strategy, **so that** I can choose with evidence and know when the index misleads.

<objective>
Apply the committed campaign rule for the first time. One path first: `make campaigns` checks the rule identity, builds the six own lists and the two followers baselines per list, computes the 18-row D-17 comparison and writes the lists, the table and the report lines. Then the per-variable objective metrics of every list, the Kohonen, Oja and PCA reading of every chosen creator (neuron, index rank, PC1/PC2) and the two figures. Last, section 5 of the deliverable: the recommendation per campaign (with the written fútbol recommendation and its assumptions) and the comparison with choosing by followers only, without return claims.

Purpose: CAMP-01 (recommendation per campaign justified with Kohonen, Oja and PCA) and CAMP-02 (comparison with the followers-only strategy at the same k: overlap@5, topic share, cost per Argentine reach).
Output: analysis/campaigns.py, analysis/tests/test_campaigns.py, the `campaigns` Makefile target, analysis/results/campaigns/, section 5 of deliverables/03-oja-campanas.md.
</objective>
