## Phase Goal

**As a** campaign analyst, **I want to** run a reproducible C/Python pipeline over an audited, standardized creadores.csv with PCA, **so that** every later result rests on justified variables.

<objective>
EDA of datos/creadores.csv (distributions, correlations, skewness, exact and near dependencies) with a generated audit table, and the written Phase 1 fragment that justifies the variable selection, the z-score and the PCA decision rule, written before the PCA is run (plan 01-08, two waves later).

Purpose: the enunciado asks to select variables with justification; the audit table is that justification, and fixing the PCA rule in writing before looking at the scree keeps the dimensionality decision honest.
Output: analysis/plots_style.py, analysis/eda.py, the prepare and eda targets in the root Makefile, deliverables/01-datos-pca.md (sections 1 to 5 and 8).
</objective>
